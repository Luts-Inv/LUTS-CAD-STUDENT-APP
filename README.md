# LUTS CAD STUDENT APP

## Engineering Design & Technology Learning Platform

LUTS CAD Student App is a web-based learning platform designed to provide
students with structured access to engineering design courses, learning
materials, practical exercises, assignments and projects.

The platform is being developed by **LUTS Investments / LUTS CAD Services**
to support practical engineering and CAD education.

---

## 🎓 Current Course

### AutoCAD — Engineering Design

The first course being developed on the platform is an engineering-focused
AutoCAD course.

Students will progress from basic CAD concepts to practical engineering
design projects.

### Course Structure

- Module 1 — Introduction to CAD
- Module 2 — Drawing Tools
- Module 3 — Modification Tools
- Module 4 — Layers & Annotation
- Module 5 — Engineering Drawings
- Module 6 — Mechanical Design
- Final Engineering Design Project

## 🎯 Long-Term Vision

The goal is to develop LUTS Student into a practical engineering education
platform covering areas such as:

- AutoCAD
- SolidWorks
- Revit
- Archicad
- Mastercam
- CAD/CAM
- Industrial Engineering
- Mechanical Design
- Engineering Simulation
- Industrial Technology

The platform will focus on **practical project-based learning**, enabling
students to progress from learning individual tools to completing complete
engineering design projects.
